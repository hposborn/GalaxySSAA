# Galaxies and the Distant Universe - Lectures 2,3,4 (Prof Hugh Osborn)

This is the material for five lectures within the "Galaxies and the Distant Universe" at the University of Bern's Space Science and Astronomy Masters:
- Spiral Galaxies and Morphological Classification
- Elliptical Galaxies, Dwarfs, and Scaling Relations
- The Evolutionary Path of Galaxies
- Chemical Enrichment and the Cosmic History of Star Formation
- The First Luminous Objects and Reionization

To see the slides go to [the associated GitHub page http://hposborn.github.io/GalaxySSAA](http://hposborn.github.io/GalaxySSAA).
